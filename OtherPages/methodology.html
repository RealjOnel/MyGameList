Integrity Protocols

What is review bombing? Review bombing is a coordinated act of posting numerous negative reviews on the site to lower the overall rating of the game. To prevent this, we use algorithms which detect, if a game gets without reason a 1/10 score for a hundred times within one hour and filters it.
